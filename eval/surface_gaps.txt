# Decision hooks the seat answers that no test yet proves asked (eval/surface_coverage.py). This list may only
# shrink: when a golden check asks one of these, the checker fails until its line is removed. Phase 1c, S3.
# hook<TAB>what proving it needs
addKeywordCost	a keyword cost choice (0..1), e.g. a card with an optional keyword cost paid on cast
announceRequirements	a ChooseAnyNumber or BidLife announcement through the engine
chooseCardsForEffectMultiple	an effect that chooses several groups of cards at once
chooseManaFromPool	paying from a pool holding more than one colour, where the colour spent matters
chooseSingleCardState	a card with two states to choose between (a double-faced or split choice)
chooseSingleReplacementEffect	two replacement effects on one event, so the order is a real choice
chooseSingleStaticAbility	two static abilities to choose between on one event
chooseSpellAbilitiesForEffect	an effect that chooses among abilities (a modal choice made by an effect)
chooseTarget	a single target chosen by an effect, not by casting
orderAttackers	attackers to order for a blocker's damage
orderBlocker	one blocker placed among blockers already ordered
payCombatCost	an attack or block tax (Propaganda, Ghostly Prison)
playTrigger	a trigger that resolves without using the stack, with a target to choose
